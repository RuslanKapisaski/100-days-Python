The best movies according to Ruslan

My top 3 movies of all time.

The Conjuring

The Conjuring (2013) is a acclaimed supernatural horror film directed by James Wan, based on the 1971 case files of real-life paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren

Inglourious Basterds

Inglourious Basterds (2009) is a 2009 epic war film written and directed by Quentin Tarantino.

The Imitation Game

The Imitation Game (2014) is a biographical thriller film about the life of mathematician Alan Turing.
